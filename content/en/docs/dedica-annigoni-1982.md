Pietro Annigoni (1910–1988), one of the greatest masters of twentieth-century Italian Realism, dedicated this autograph inscription with an original sketch to Antonio Occhipinti during a Florentine encounter in November 1982.

![Autograph dedication by Pietro Annigoni to Antonio Occhipinti, with sketch, 1982](https://res.cloudinary.com/ds8hmzyom/image/upload/v1777663278/pyartist/web/uncategorized/dedica-di-annigoni-con-schizzo-30.jpg)

> To Antonio Occhipinti, cordially and with best wishes for good work.

_**Pietro Annigoni**, 11-1982_
