The work **Preparatory study for the head of Saint Matthew** (watercolour on paper, 50×35 cm, 1996) by Antonio Occhipinti is published in the catalogue of the international and European watercolour exhibition **«Colori sull'acqua»** (Colours on Water), held in Maccagno in 2003.

The exhibition was curated by the **Associazione Italiana Acquerellisti (A.I.A.)**, the Italian Watercolourists Association, in collaboration with the **Pro Loco di Maccagno**.
