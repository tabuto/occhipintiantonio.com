Catalogue of the **International Prize Fabriano — Watercolour and Engraving Section** exhibition, held at the Oratorio del Gonfalone, Fabriano, 1987.

Antonio Occhipinti participated with his own works in this important national exhibition dedicated to watercolour and engraving.

**SBN:** ANA0010998

## The report in Secolo d'Italia

The newspaper _Secolo d'Italia_ (Friday 19 June 1987, p. 5) reported on the award ceremony of the 11th edition of the Premio Internazionale Fabriano, held at the Museo della Carta in Fabriano before numerous Italian and foreign artists, art critics and the entire management of the Cartiere Miliani Fabriano paper mill. The event, sponsored by the paper mill and organised by CIPRA of Rome, had invited 150 artists of every nationality, divided into the watercolour and engraving sections set out in the competition rules.

**The Jury**, chaired by ing. Mario Tommasini (director of Cartiere Fabriano), was composed of:

- **Dante Bernini** — Superintendent of Historical and Artistic Heritage of Rome
- **Luigi Tallarico** — art critic
- **Antonino De Bono** — art critic
- **Walter Piacesi** — artist
- **Aldo Casanova** — Jury secretary

After examining the works admitted to the competition, the Jury awarded the two «Trofei Fabriano 1987» (a work by the Volterra sculptor Tiburzi) to **Antonio Occhipinti of Gela**, for the watercolour section, and to Roberto Stelluti of Fabriano, for the engraving section.
