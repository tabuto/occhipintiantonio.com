Participation in the **21st international watercolour salon** organised by the Aquarel Instituut van België (AIB), formerly the Kempische Aquarelvereniging (founded 1976). The event was held at 't Kristallijn, Blauwe Keidreef 3, Mol (Belgium), in March 1999.

In 1999 the institute adopted the international name AIB with the aim of broadening its European reach.
