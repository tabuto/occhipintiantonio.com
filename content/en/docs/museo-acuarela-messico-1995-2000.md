The **Museo Nacional de la Acuarela «Alfredo Guati Rojo»**, located in Coyoacán, Mexico City, is Mexico's foremost museum institution dedicated to watercolour. Founded by the watercolourist [Alfredo Guati Rojo](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfredo_Guati_Rojo), it is an international reference point for the art of watercolour painting.

In May 2026, Dr Scarlet Galindo Monteagudo, Director of the museum, confirmed — following a specific search of the institution's pictorial and documentary archive — Antonio Occhipinti's participation in two international exhibitions hosted by the museum.

---

## Communication from the Museum Director

![Communication from the Director of the Museo Nacional de la Acuarela «Alfredo Guati Rojo», Dr Scarlet Galindo Monteagudo, May 2026](https://res.cloudinary.com/ds8hmzyom/image/upload/v1782296300/pyartist/web/varie/email-fwdinformacionsobreantonioocchipinti-20260514-39.jpg)

### Original text (Spanish)

> **Estimado Francesco di Dio:**
>
> Primero que nada, disculpa la demora, te comento que después de la revisión en nuestro archivo pictórico y documental, te comparto que el artista Antonio Occhipintini participó en exposiciones del museo, no contamos con obra de él en nuestra colección, te mando documentos relacionados con las exposiciones y obras con las que participó en exposiciones.
>
> Quedamos al pendiente de tus comentarios.

_Dr **Scarlet Galindo Monteagudo**, Director — Museo Nacional de la Acuarela «Alfredo Guati Rojo», Salvador Novo 88, Coyoacán_

This message followed an internal note from the museum's Conservation and Mounting Department (22 April 2026), written by Arturo Mondragón Téllez:

> Le escribo para mandar la información correspondiente de las participaciones de Antonio Occhipintini en el MUNACUA, no he encontrado que tengamos obra de él en el acervo, ni registro de donación de obra en el archivo histórico, solo participación en dos exposiciones: Exposición de la Asociación Italiana de Acuarelistas (1995) y IV Bienal Internacional (2000).

### English translation

> **Dear Francesco di Dio,**
>
> First of all, I apologise for the delay. Following a review of our pictorial and documentary archive, I can confirm that the artist Antonio Occhipinti participated in exhibitions at the museum. We do not hold any of his works in our collection, but I am sending you the documents relating to the exhibitions and to the works he presented.
>
> We remain available for any further comments.

_Dr **Scarlet Galindo Monteagudo**, Director — Museo Nacional de la Acuarela «Alfredo Guati Rojo», Salvador Novo 88, Coyoacán_

Internal note from the Conservation and Mounting Department (22 April 2026, signed by Arturo Mondragón Téllez):

> I am writing to send you information concerning Antonio Occhipinti's participations at MUNACUA. I have not found any works by him in the collection, nor any record of a donation in the historical archive; only participation in two exhibitions: Exhibition of the Italian Association of Watercolourists (1995) and IV International Watercolour Biennial (2000). I enclose the relevant information and the exhibition catalogue scans.
