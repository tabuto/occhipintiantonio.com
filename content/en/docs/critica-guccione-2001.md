Critical text handwritten by [Piero Guccione](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piero_Guccione), September 2001.

> Antonio Occhipinti's watercolours have the freshness of simple things: yet their path is far from simple, even from a technical point of view.
>
> Occhipinti is a quick and attentive draughtsman, and it is on this basis that his ability to capture the light in his landscapes is swift and joyful: joyful as the gaze that admires them.
>
> I know that our times do not favour the skill and mastery of those who know how to do — at least as far as figurative art is concerned.
>
> But in the absence, in the emptiness of purpose, in the globalisation of nothingness that today's society proposes, skill and mastery — as in Occhipinti's case — become certain values of reference.

_**Piero Guccione**, September 2001_
