The Prior of the Basilica of San Lorenzo in Florence, Monsignor Angelo Livi, wrote this autograph letter on 25 July 1984 to thank Antonio Occhipinti for the gift of a watercolour, praising his technique just as master Pietro Annigoni had done.

![Autograph letter from the Prior of the Basilica of S. Lorenzo, Florence, 1984](https://res.cloudinary.com/ds8hmzyom/image/upload/v1777651226/pyartist/web/varie/lettera-basilica-slorenzo-firenze-1994-26.jpg)

```
INSIGNE BASILICA DI S. LORENZO
FIRENZE
```

> Dear Sir,
>
> This morning Signora Martinelli brought me one of your paintings, a watercolour you painted. I bow before your skill and technique, as our great Annigoni himself praised.
>
> Signora Martinelli tells me it is a tribute, a gift from you.
>
> I hasten to thank you with all my heart. Here in S. Lorenzo we safeguard priceless artistic treasures from past centuries; this painting cannot hang in the church, but resting in my rectory it will be admired by many: my home sees a constant flow of visitors.
>
> Thank you very much. I regret that I was away from Florence during the exhibition. But there will be other occasions. I wish you great fortune in painting and… in life.
>
> With deepest regards and a grateful heart,

_The Prior **Msgr. Angelo Livi**_
